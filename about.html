About DuckWorth's Shine Co.

A locally owned detailing business built around one idea: do the job properly, or don't do it at all.

Our Story

Started in Battle Creek

DuckWorth's Shine Co. is a detailing business based at 1350 E Columbia Ave in Battle Creek, Michigan, serving Calhoun County and the surrounding area. We offer shop detailing at our facility, on-location boat detailing, and heavy-duty cleaning for work trucks and commercial fleets.

We started this business with a simple observation: most people don't have time to sit around a waiting room for half a day just to get their vehicle cleaned. We also noticed that boat owners and work truck operators get left out of most detailing shops entirely — even though their vehicles need the same care.

So we built a business around it. Shop detailing for cars, boats, and trucks that want a full transformation. On-location work for boats at the dock or on a trailer, and for work trucks and fleets that can't afford downtime.

What We Do

Our Services

Three focused services, done properly. Every job starts with a quick look at the vehicle or boat so we can quote a fair price before we begin.

Shop Detailing

Full interior and exterior detailing performed at our Battle Creek facility. Ideal for deep cleans and full transformations.

Boat Detailing

Hull cleaning, oxidation removal, gel coat protection, and interior detail for boats of all sizes. On-location work available.

Work Truck & Fleet

Heavy-duty cleaning for commercial vehicles, work trucks, vans, and fleets. Recurring service available.
