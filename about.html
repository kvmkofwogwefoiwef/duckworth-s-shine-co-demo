About DuckWorth's Shine Co.

A locally owned shop and mobile detailing business built around one idea: do the job properly, or don't do it at all.

Our Story

Started in Battle Creek

DuckWorth's Shine Co. is a detailing business based at 1350 E Columbia Ave in Battle Creek, Michigan, serving Calhoun County and the surrounding area. We offer both shop detailing at our facility and mobile service at your home, dock, or jobsite.

We started this business with a simple observation: most people don't have time to sit around a waiting room for half a day just to get their vehicle cleaned. We also noticed that boat owners and work truck operators get left out of most detailing shops entirely — even though their vehicles need the same care.

So we built a business around it. Shop detailing for anyone who wants to bring their vehicle to us. Mobile detailing for anyone who'd rather we come to them. Boats, trucks, and fleets — we handle them all.
