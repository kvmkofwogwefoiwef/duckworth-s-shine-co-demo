About DuckWorth's Shine Co.

A locally owned mobile and shop detailing business in Battle Creek, built to make professional detailing convenient for the people who need it.

Our Story

Started in Battle Creek

DuckWorth's Shine Co. is an auto detailing and mobile detailing business serving Battle Creek, Michigan, and the surrounding area. We bring professional detailing to your location — or you can bring your vehicle to us.

We started this business with a simple observation: getting your car detailed usually means giving up half a day. You drop it off, wait around, then figure out how to get back. We thought there was a better way — so we built a service that comes to you.

We're a small, hands-on operation. When you book with us, you're working directly with the two owners, Guillermo and Dean. No call centers, no automated systems, no layers between you and the person holding the polisher.

What We Do

Our Services

We offer four core services — all available as mobile or shop work, depending on what you need.

Mobile Detailing

We come to you. Full interior and exterior service at your home, office, or jobsite.

Shop Detailing

Complete interior and exterior care at our Battle Creek facility — ideal for larger jobs that need more space and equipment.

Rim & Tire Detailing

Wheel deep-clean, brake dust removal, tire dressing, and shine. Standalone or as an add-on.

Work Truck & Fleet

Heavy-duty cleaning packages designed for commercial vehicles, work trucks, and vans.
