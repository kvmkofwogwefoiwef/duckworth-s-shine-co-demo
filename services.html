  • Wheels, tires, and wheel wells cleaned
  • Iron and tar decontamination
  • Clay bar treatment
  • Interior vacuum and extraction
  • Leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Streak-free interior and exterior glass
  • Hand-applied sealant for protection

Service 02

Boat Detailing

Full hull cleaning, oxidation removal, and interior detailing for boats of all sizes. We treat gel coat the way it deserves — with marine-grade products that protect against sun, water spots, and Michigan lake grime.

Available at our shop or as mobile service — we can come to your dock or driveway.

  • Hull wash and water spot removal
  • Oxidation removal and light compounding
  • Gel coat sealant and marine-grade wax
  • Deck, gunwale, and non-skid cleaning
  • Interior, cabin, and upholstery detail
  • Vinyl and leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Bilge, livewell, and compartment cleaning
  • Metal polishing (stainless, aluminum, chrome)

  • Full hull wash and water spot removal
  • Light oxidation removal and compounding

Work Truck & Commercial Fleet Detailing

Heavy-duty cleaning and restoration packages tailored specifically for commercial vehicles and work trucks. Keep your fleet presentable and protect your investment.

Mobile service available — we come to your yard or jobsite. Shop service also available.
