Detailing Services

Mobile and shop service. Work truck ready. Message us on social to coordinate your appointment.

Service 01

Mobile Detailing

On-site full-service cleaning and detailing brought directly to your location. We bring the water, power, and professional products — you don't have to go anywhere.

  • Full exterior hand wash and dry
  • Interior vacuum, wipe-down, and glass cleaning
  • Wheel and tire cleaning with dressing
  • Door jambs and trunk seals
  • Perfect for homes, offices, and apartment complexes
  • Serving Battle Creek and surrounding Calhoun County

Service 02

Shop Detailing

Complete interior and exterior care performed at our dedicated facility. Best for full transformations, multi-stage work, and vehicles that need extra attention.

  • Deep interior cleaning and extraction
  • Full exterior wash, decontamination, and sealant
  • Leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Odor treatment and deodorizing
  • Controlled lighting for quality inspection
  • Ideal for pre-sale preparation and full resets

Service 03

Rim & Tire Detailing

Targeted deep cleaning, brake dust removal, and tire shining using step-by-step professional techniques. Your wheels are the first thing people notice.

  • Wheel face and barrel deep cleaning
  • Brake dust and iron fallout removal
  • Tire sidewall cleaning and degreasing
  • Professional tire dressing and shine
  • Wheel well cleaning
  • Available as add-on or standalone service

Service 04

Work Truck & Commercial Fleet Detailing

Heavy-duty cleaning and restoration packages tailored specifically for commercial vehicles and work trucks. Keep your fleet presentable and protect your investment.

  • Heavy-duty exterior cleaning for trucks and vans
  • Cab interior cleaning and organization
  • Bed cleaning and treatment options
  • Recurring service programs available
  • Volume pricing based on fleet size
  • Mobile service available for fleet accounts
