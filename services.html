Detailing Services

Shop, boat, and fleet detailing done properly. Every job starts with a quick assessment so we can quote a fair price before we begin.

Service 01

Shop Detailing

Complete interior and exterior care performed at our dedicated facility. Best for full transformations, deep cleaning, and vehicles that need extra attention.

  • Deep interior cleaning and extraction
  • Full exterior wash, decontamination, and sealant
  • Leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Odor treatment and deodorizing
  • Controlled lighting for quality inspection
  • Ideal for pre-sale preparation and full resets

Service 02

Boat Detailing

Full hull cleaning, oxidation removal, and interior detailing for boats of all sizes. We treat gel coat the way it deserves — with marine-grade products that protect against sun, water spots, and Michigan lake grime.

  • Hull wash and water spot removal
  • Oxidation removal and light compounding
  • Gel coat sealant and marine-grade wax
  • Deck, gunwale, and non-skid cleaning
  • Interior, cabin, and upholstery detail
  • Vinyl and leather cleaning and conditioning
  • Bilge, livewell, and compartment cleaning
  • Metal polishing (stainless, aluminum, chrome)
